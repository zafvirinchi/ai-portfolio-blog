## Question

What is Microservices Architecture?

## Answer

Microservices architecture is an approach where an application is divided into small, independent services.

Each service owns a specific business capability and can be developed, deployed and scaled independently.

## Example

An e-commerce system can have:

- User Service
- Order Service
- Payment Service
- Notification Service

## Interview Explanation

Microservices improve scalability and flexibility, but they also add complexity in communication, deployment, monitoring and data consistency.
